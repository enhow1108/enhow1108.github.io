隱私權政策 · Privacy Policy

最後更新 · Last updated: 2026-10-13

中文

family-agent 是擁有者自行架設、僅供自家使用的應用程式，沒有其他使用者。

  • 存取的 Google 資料：只使用 Google Drive 的 drive.file 權限，用來上傳、列出與刪除這個應用程式自己建立的備份檔。它無法讀取 Drive 中的其他檔案。
  • 資料用途：備份檔只用於在資料遺失時還原應用程式自己的資料。
  • 儲存位置：資料存放在擁有者自己的電腦與擁有者自己的 Google Drive。
  • 分享：不會把任何資料提供、出售或轉移給第三方，也不用於廣告。
  • 刪除與撤銷：擁有者可隨時刪除 Drive 中的備份檔，或在 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式存取權」中撤銷授權。

English

family-agent is a self-hosted application used only by its owner's household. It has no other users.

  • Google data accessed: only the Google Drive drive.file scope, used to upload, list and delete backup files that this application itself creates. It cannot read any other files in Drive.
  • Use of data: backup files are used solely to restore the application's own data after a loss.
  • Storage: data stays on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and none is used for advertising.
  • Deletion and revocation: the owner can delete the backup files from Drive at any time, or revoke access under “Third-party apps with account access” in their Google Account.
