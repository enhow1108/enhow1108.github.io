family-agent

family-agent 是一個自用的家庭助理，幫忙記錄孩子的閱讀、規劃週末活動。它不對外提供服務，只有擁有者一家人使用。

family-agent is a private household assistant for tracking children's reading and planning weekends. It is not offered to the public; the only users are the owner's family.

Google Drive 的用途 · Use of Google Drive

這個應用程式每天把自己的資料庫備份上傳到擁有者本人的 Google Drive。它只要求 drive.file 權限，因此只能存取它自己建立的備份檔，無法讀取 Drive 中的其他檔案。

The app uploads a daily backup of its own database to the owner's Google Drive. It requests only the drive.file scope, so it can access only the backup files it creates and nothing else in Drive.
